Version 3.01 (Old Version)
Ship Balance & Reworks
Rime M:

Swapped medium missile slots to Composites.

Increased base speed from 65 to 95.

Increased flux dissipation from 300 to 350.

Increased flux capacity from 6000 to 7000.

Design Note: The Rime was significantly inferior to the Falcon despite costing more DP, leaving it outmatched by the Gust. This shift gives it a solid role as a cheaper brawler and support hull with two fighter bays.

Coanda:

Added a built-in Micro Missile Array.

Swapped the rear hybrid slot to a Universal slot.

Design Note: The Coanda was aggressively mediocre. These built-in utilities give it a distinct identity and a real reason to be fielded.

Pandemonium: Increased deployment cost from 65 to 75 DP.

Weapon Balance & Reworks
Srab Heavy Emitter:

Increased damage to 750, and increased projectile health to 600.

Increased projectile speed to 550 for better responsiveness.

Enhanced sound effects by 0.2s to give the firing audio more punch.

Design Note: These changes make the weapon punchy and reactive enough to bypass competent enemy point defense grids.

Grave HMC:

Increased damage to 70.

Design Note: This weapon now functions as a longer-ranged heavy machine gun, trading away flux efficiency for superior default reach and solid hull damage.

Recson V:

Increased damage from 200 to 225, set flux per shot to 225, and increased the refire delay to 2.4s.

Design Note: Positioned as a middle ground between the Heavy Mass Driver and the Hypervelocity Driver, offering higher DPS and better flux efficiency than the HVD, but with worse overall accuracy.

State Support Beam:

Completely reworked into a short-duration burst beam with a high rate of fire. It excels at shredding armor, but it features terrible flux efficiency and handles poorly against shields.

Lazyhorn: Decreased OP cost from 12 to 10 to make it a more attractive point defense choice.

Recson S: Decreased OP cost to 6 to ensure it can properly compete with the Light Mass Driver and Railgun alternatives.
