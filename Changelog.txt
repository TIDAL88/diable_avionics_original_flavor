Ships

Weapons
* IBIS and Raptor full revert:
   * Refire delay: 0.05 → 0.10s (peak dps is halved,but can fire for twice as long on the same magazine)
   * Projectile speed: 2000 → 1000
   * IBIS turn speed: 180 → 90°/s
   * Raptor turn speed: 180 → 75°/s, matching the Vulcan.
   * Raptor ammo capacity: 64 → 48
   * Ibis magazine now lasts twice as long.
   * Raptor magazine now lasts 50% longer.
   * Reasoning: Restoring Tartiflette’s original profile reins in their excessive burst damage while preserving their magazine fed character, giving both weapons a longer and more deliberate firing cycle.
* Banish Torpedoes (Applies to both small and medium variants)
   * Actual damage and EMP restored from `1,500 → 2,000`, matching the tooltip. Bugfix resulting in a buff.
   * Warhead turn rate increased from `90 → 120°/s`.
   * Turn acceleration increased from `90 → 180°/s²`.
   * Custom non-ECCM target-leading penalty removed entirely.
   * Minimum aiming time reduced: Adjusted the launch animation for a punchier release.
      * Without ECCM: 1.5 → 1 second
      * With ECCM: 0.75 → 0.6 seconds
   * Reasoning: The hidden target leading debuff came from an old custom ECCM check that predates vanilla’s current bonuses. It basically turned ECCM into an on/off switch for target leading. That’s gone. ECCM is still advised to make the torpedo get past defenses more often, but it is no longer required for the torpedo to aim properly. The faster rotation is mostly a visual feedback/game-feel tweak for today’s starsector. ECCM will still make the aim animation faster, as intended by its original designt.
* Versant - Harvest Burst Mode: Damage per shot reduced from 150 to 100, lowering combined DPS from 900 to 600 with a 420 flux/second consumption.
   * Reasoning: Restores the original intent of three distinct profiles: Automatic for sustained fire and EMP, Burst for accurate and efficient volleys, and Semi-auto for long-range, high-damage shots.
* Micromissile reload sizes normalized to clean multiples of burst size: Negligible changes for the player, more about having clean stats for easier balancing in the future.
* Pandemonium Micromissile Arrays: Magazine capacity reverted from 300 to 20. This is not a nerf: their regeneration fully sustains continuous firing after the Micromissile revert, just like it did originally. The oversized magazine served no practical purpose.
* Plover: increased volume very slightly for feedback during combat.
* Deep Strike Catapult : Added the “Deep Strike” role and clarified its reload cycle in the tooltip.

Weapon Balance:

The Grand Goal: “We want to drown the player in tactical choices” could be the motto of this patch. Rather than gutting the gear that is already meta, we looked at creating more synergies and making unused weapons more viable, completing what was started last patch.

Hexafire Scatter Cannon:

The infamous Hexafire was a weapon that suffered from many issues and struggled heavily to compete against other large weapons in the Diable Avionics armory, as well as vanilla weapons like the Plasma Cannon. This gun has received major changes to carve out a proper role for itself.

Twin Hexafire Scatter Cannon:
Increased EMP from 30 to 50.
Reduced chargedown from 2.5s to 1.25s.
Impact: These changes turn the Twin Hexafire into a powerful anti-swarm weapon capable of handling hordes of low-survivability ships, while its EMP damage keeps it effective against tougher opponents at high flux. It now fires twice as fast, greatly increasing both its DPS and flux consumption equally. It remains highly ineffective against heavy armor due to pitiful hit-strength, and its unchanged spread pattern makes recoil management even more important at this fire rate. As with the Uhlan, pilots will need to exercise trigger discipline to maintain accuracy.
Hexafire Scatter Cannon (Standard):
Reduced flux cost per shot from 100 to 80.
Reduced chargedown from 2.5s to 1.25s.
Impact: The smaller Hexafire is similar to the twin version, albeit with better efficiency. It remains a decent generalist medium energy weapon that doesn’t like heavy armor.

State support beam:
In the early days of Diable Avionics, the state was designed as a Phase Lance-like dealing Energy damage. After it was converted into a High Explosive weapon in later patches, its double-beam structure and exaggerated wiggling began interfering with damage calculations and armor shred. Its practical performance rarely matched the numbers shown to the player, making the weapon difficult to understand and build around.
Numbers balance:
Increased beam dps from 800 to 1,000.
Increased flux cost from 1,000 to 1,300 per second.
Increased the cooldown from 0.8s to 5s.
Reduced chargedown from 1s to 0.1s.
Reduced turn rate from 30 to 12 to match the Nocturne.
Now recognized as a single beam by the game that means 2.5x hit strength compared to last version due to the reworked beam under the hood.
VFX and Animation Overhaul:
Completely redone the weapon’s vfx and beam sprites so that you don’t notice we’ve redone the weapon’s vfx and beam sprites.
The two visible beams now are actually a single beam (it’s a secret). The weapon still looks and feels like the old double-beam emitter, but its displayed damage, actual damage, and armor shred now make sense.
Slightly reduced the beam’s signature wiggle, making its damage application more consistent without removing the weapon’s distinctive movement.
Impact: The State is now a predictable, high-intensity anti-armor burst weapon. Its higher damage is balanced by increased flux consumption and considerably longer downtime between bursts. And the new visuals build on previously established art direction.

Nocturne Emitter: Decreased OP cost by 1, and reduced burst delay by 1.5 seconds.
Reasoning: More DPS allows it to act as a proper combination of the Ion Beam and Graviton Beam in a bursty lance form.
Increased volume just a smidge (did you ever hear the Nocturne in combat?)

Uhlan Siege Laser: Reduced minimum spread from 6 to 3.

It retains its temperamental recoil, so subsequent shots will be the Uhlan you are used to, requiring either patience or good grazer timing.

Artassault Revolver Cannon: Increased damage by 5 (matching the LAG flux efficiency buff from vanilla).

Wanzers
The Grand Goal: The objective of these changes is to eliminate the “Best Wanzer” or “Blizz/Raven/Whatever meta” phenomenon. All Wanzers are now equally viable for their OP cost, featuring clear roles and strategies. You are free to explore creative synergies between certain Wanzers rather than just spamming a single "best" option.
Don’t forget about supportive and disruptive Wanzers, especially when running non-gantry bays. We didn’t touch those because the Valiant, Strife, and Hoar are still super strong at their jobs, and the Frosts remain the cost-efficiency kings (especially when coupled with a wing that can protect them).

Blizzaia: Increased OP cost to 18 (up from 16). Grave LAC damage reduced to 45 (down from 70). 
Impact: It has been the best overall Wanzer for a while now, especially thanks to its synergy of having PD, AoE High Explosive for its allies, and the best flares in the game. The main issue was its kinetic damage, which made the wing too oppressive when spammed. It could do everything: out-DPS a shield next to a Warlust, or maintain a higher chance of overloading the enemy than a Raven. It is still the same angry guy with a grenade launcher at the end of the day and keeps its first volley damage; it just won’t out-DPS a shield next to an Avalanche or beat a Raven/Zephyr on hull damage anymore. We want you to be creative and find Wanzer combinations that fit the enemy or your fleet doctrine, rather than just spamming the Blizz against everything.

Avalanche: Decreased OP cost to 18 (down from 19), and increased top speed from 165 to 190, its maneuverability has been buffed to be higher than the Blizzaia but below a Warlust.
Reasoning: Our shock trooper was a little too slow, considering the Avalanche gets no PD and no shields, and gets slowed by its main gun’s recoil.
Snowball Shotgun: Set damage type to Energy, and increased damage per shot from 350 to 450.
Snowball Shotgun Projectiles: Set baseline projectile type to Energy, and increased damage from 65 to 75.
Berserker now also momentarily increases those projectiles’ damage to 84. 

Impact: The Avalanche and the Blizzaia are much more closely matched now. The shotgun dealing energy damage means it deals full damage to shields. It is now a better alternative to the Blizzy when you need to kill targets with high shields but lower armor. Try fielding them against High Tech; they will also synergize together beautifully now that the avalanche loses its armor shred in exchange for all of this.

Zephyr: Increased Micro Glaux damage to 500/s, set Aether Splitter chest beam damage to 1500/s, and increased base OP cost from 17 to 22.
Impact: The Zephyr is now a true multirole 9th-generation Wanzer that has a Super-Interceptor kick to it. Because it only has access to soft flux damage, it is not as good as Ravens at busting ships on its own, nor is it as good as Hoars and Strifes for PD or anti-fighter duty. However, it is capable of performing both roles effectively without stepping on the toes of specialist Wanzers, all while being surprisingly hard to kill thanks to its shield. Now, it packs terrifying hull damage when the conditions are right. Some might call it a Super-Valiant that trades the disruption capabilities and speed of its little brother for pure combat prowess.

Warlust: DMR range increased to 800 (up from 700).
Impact: This minor change has made a surprising impact on the Warlust’s damage charts; it finally lives up to its "sniper" moniker.

Hoar: Increased OP cost to 12 (up from 10), increased refit delay to 13 (from 12).
Impact: This puts the Hoar and Strife at the same OP cost and refit delay, giving players a choice between the on-target PD space superiority of the Strife, or the bodyguard duties around the mothership offered by the Hoar. The Hoar received this nerf due to being extremely effective at deleting all enemy wings near its mothership while simultaneously providing EMP.

Raven: Reduced HP from 2000 to 1750, and removed 2 charges from the Light Flicker Core (down to 2 from 4).

Impact: The Raven has historically been one of the most overperforming wings in the entire Diable Avionics roster. A generous amount of HP and 300 armor made these Wanzers tough nuts to crack while dealing massive damage. Furthermore, their ability to teleport made them very difficult to punish and highly dangerous offensively, allowing them to blink behind ships to take out unprotected engines or bypass non-360° shields. The point of this nerf is to give the Raven distinct weaknesses without making it unusable. It is still quite powerful and still is best in class in certain situations, but it is no longer a "spam this only and win" Wanzer.

Micro Missile Overhaul (some would call it a revert):

Micro Missiles are one of Diable Avionics’ defining weapon systems. Between versions 2.8.0 and 2.8.4, successive changes moved them away from Flashfrozen and Tartiflette’s original design. Iteration is not inherently bad, but here the cumulative buffs produced endless waves of durable missiles that could overwhelm Starsector’s AI. In late-game playtests, this repeatedly caused enemy fleets to break down against Diable, making victory far too reliable with little investment in missile enhancements. This overhaul returns Micro Missiles to their original philosophy: fewer missiles, moving faster and hitting harder, with less durability and saturation potential.

Global Projectile Changes
Range: 2,000 → 1,500
Damage: 50 → 100 High Explosive
Projectile speed: 200 → 300
Missile hitpoints: 50 → 25
Flight time: 18 → 12 seconds. Daze exception: 15 seconds (because of uniquely slow daze animation)

Launcher Changes
Lower burst counts come with shorter refire delays: the standalone cycle drops from 6.0 to 3.0 seconds, and the major built-in arrays also speed up. The goal is smaller, more frequent salvos, not lost uptime. The flux values have been equalized to be the same flux per second as what they were last patch.
Equippable and built-in launchers: burst 3 → 1; flux 10 → 15; refire 5.7 → 2.9 seconds; cycle 6.0 → 3.0 seconds.

Pocket Gust cluster: burst 8 → 6; full cycle 7.0 → 6.8 seconds.
Storm cluster: burst 10 → 6; minor flux increase; full cycle 7.2 → 4.7 seconds.
Coanda cluster: burst remains at 6; full cycle remains at 6.8 seconds.
Daze cluster: burst 5 → 3; minor flux increase; full cycle 6.7 → 4.6 seconds.
Maelstrom array: burst 40 → 20; flux per missile 10 → 15; full cycle 8.0 → 6.0 seconds.
Pandemonium/station arrays: burst 40 → 10; flux 10 → 25; full cycle 8.0 → 5.0 seconds.

Impact
Micro Missiles remain powerful pressure tools: they distract Point Defense, create openings for Wanzers, and threaten exposed armor. Hits matter more, but smaller, more fragile waves give dedicated Point Defense a meaningful response. The standard launcher fires twice as often, while faster built-in arrays offset their lower burst counts. Micro Missiles stay dependable without creating a permanent cloud; timing and coordination matter more than raw saturation.

Virtuous Ship Systems Rebalance
Brawler:
The new brawler has more weight and inertia, effectively resulting in a 10% nerf to the total distance traveled during the ship system.
In return, the ship system remains active for an extra 0.5s. You’re not as zippy, but you can pilot the ship system for longer.
Aims to give it a more visceral gamefeel in line with the trailer/OG release, while remaining at the same power level as current releases thanks to animation and numbers trickery. These temporal shell numbers and the increased duration mean it will retain 99% of the damage potential of other current forks.
Since the skill now lasts longer, its total cooldown has been raised to 15.625 from 15.15, so it will feel familiar if you are used to the rhythm of the Brawler.
TL;DR: 10% mobility nerf, improved feel, same effective DPS.

Skirmisher:
Returned to Tart’s animations and ship system for a more readable and clear experience in combat. Removed feature-creep: it no longer automatically deletes all projectiles within a 1000 radius or grants free damage and EMP. In short, it is no longer so OP that you'll feel guilty about playing it. It's back to being the ultimate opportunistic mobility core.

Recon:
Grants an active 0-flux speed boost as long as the ship generates below 10% of its flux cap per second. This is intended to incentivize playing on the edge of max range, recon-style, dipping in and out of firing range rather than playing as a brawler.

Guardian:
Untouched.

Virtuous Weapons Rebalance
Glaux mk2: Increased damage, but increased flux consumption equally.

Reasoning: It is no longer a weapon you can just fire without keeping sflux or venting in mind, though it remains far more flux-efficient than a Scicle. Bass and volume have also been increased.

Snowblast Shotgun: Reduced range to 700 from 900. Increased magazine size and damage in return, reloading more rounds but with a slower reload time.

Reasoning: In its previous state, the Roar Gatling was the go-to kinetic choice, and the Snowblast shotgun was only better when used on the Brawler. We aimed to sacrifice some range to make it more viable outside of Brawler configurations, especially when utilizing the classic manual aim mechanic (read the tooltip).

Grasshopper Grenade Launcher: Added 1 extra ammo to the magazine, and it now reloads 6 rounds instead of 5. Reloads slightly faster now.
Reasoning: This makes it less reliant on Missile Specialization, rendering it a little more viable before you unlock all skills. The weapon will still require careful planning and trigger discipline if you don’t want to end up without grenades after your first kill, the reload time is still longer than anything in vanilla. It remains a skill-check weapon that heavily favors manual aim when piloting.

Beast Cannon: Added 25 damage, bringing damage per round up from 325 to 350.
Reasoning: This is a simple buff to ensure the Beast/non-missile builds are not completely outmatched by the Grasshopper.

Added Ship Variants

Pandemonium (Assault):
While the Pandemonium-class dreadnought is an extremely powerful ship, it lacks a "fun" feel. It is slow and not as heavily armed with big guns as one would hope for such a massive vessel. To rectify this, the legacy version of the Pandemonium with 4 large turrets has been added.

Reasoning:
To diversify the current version from the new one, a special hullmod has been added for both versions, aiming to make them more interesting while slightly increasing their power level without overdoing it.
The Assault Configuration hullmod is tailored specifically to this version of the Pandemonium: it reduces your range and weapon damage, but increases your rate of fire, reduces the OP cost of medium and small weapons, and increases your top speed. This hullmod differentiates both versions into distinct niches where they can excel.

Pandemonium (Artillery):
The legacy version of the Pandemonium, this time equipped with 4 large mounts. Those who enjoy fielding Uhlans will love this version.

The Artillery Configuration is a hullmod similar to its assault counterpart but features the opposite buffs and downsides. Namely, it grants lower speed and a lower rate of fire, but increases range, slightly boosts damage, and reduces the OP cost of large weapons.

Hullmods
Diable Aesthetics: Lore accurate cosmetic hullmod that replicates Advanced Avionics afterimage trails on Diable ships. No bonuses, no cost. 
The hullmod will automatically appear in your hullmod choices pool if you are refitting a Diable ship, no need to hunt for it. 
Color picker : Pick between the classic advanced avionics trail in red or the stock Diable engine teal.
